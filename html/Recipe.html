Classic Vegetable sandwich

A quick, healthy and delicious sandwich loaded with fresh vegetables and flavour

Recipe Information

  • Prepration Time:10 minutes
  • Cooking Time:5 minutes
  • Servings:2

Ingredients

  • 4 slices of bread
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tomato (sliced)
  • 1 small onion (sliced)
  • 1 cucumber (sliced)
  • 2 cheese slices
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

instructions

  1. Apply butter on one side of all the bread slices.
  2. Place tomato, onion and cucmber slices on two bread slices.
  3. Sprinkle salt and black pepper on top.
  4. Add cheese slices.
  5. Cover with the remaining bread slices
  6. Grill or toast the sandwich untill it turns golden brown
  7. Cut and serve hot

Nutrition Information (per serving)

Nutrition Info
Nutrient Amount
Calories 250 kcal
Protien 8 g
Carbohydrates 30 g
Fat 10 g
Fiber 3 g

Tip

Toasting the bread lightly makes the sandwich crispier and enhances the flavour.
